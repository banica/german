# Call for Papers

We invite researchers, practitioners, and students to submit their work for the **German Model 101 Workshop**.

## Topics of Interest
We welcome submissions related to:
- Dual education systems
- Vocational training comparisons
- German economic models
- Cross-cultural management
- Industry 4.0 in Germany and Romania

## Important Dates
- **Abstract Submission:** October 15, 2025
- **Notification of Acceptance:** November 1, 2025
- **Camera-Ready Copy:** November 15, 2025

## Submission Guidelines
Please submit a 1-page abstract (PDF format) via our [Submission Portal](#).
All submissions will be peer-reviewed by the Program Committee.
