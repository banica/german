# German Model 101 Workshop

Welcome to the official website of the **German Model 101 Workshop**.

## About the Workshop

The **German Model 101 Workshop** is an event dedicated to students, young professionals, and enthusiasts of German education and culture. The workshop aims to provide an in-depth understanding of the German model, with practical examples and relevant applications in education, industry, and culture.

### Vision

- **Develop** participants' knowledge of the German model.
- **Foster** collaboration between young people and professionals from Romania and Germany.
- **Build** practical and cultural skills applicable in education and industry.
- **Strengthen** cultural and professional ties between Germany and Romania.

## Key Topics

- Structure of the German education and vocational training system
- Industrial and Economic Best Practices in Germany
- Case studies and models applicable in Romania
- Cultural Exchange and Professional Networking
- Collaborative projects and internship opportunities
